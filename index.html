How we work

From a phone to a printed card.

  1. 01

    Students send their details from their phones.

    We share one form link with your school. Students or parents add their details and a photo in a minute. Or send us your Excel sheet.

  2. 02

    Every detail is checked.

    Spellings, classes, roll numbers, blood groups and photos are checked against your school's list. Anything wrong comes back to you to fix.

  3. 03

    You approve every card.

    See a proof of each card exactly as it will be printed. Nothing is printed until you say yes.

  4. 04

    Printed, finished, delivered.

    Cards come with lanyards and holders, ready to hand out on the first day.
